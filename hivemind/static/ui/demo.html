HiveMind top bar

Inline SVG, animated in CSS. The bar above is the real thing — the same markup templates/_topbar.html renders into the app.

States

Idle: soft bubbling, the odd spark crossing a trace. Active: dense bubbling, the brain pulses, charge runs continuously from the board through the cables into the jar's base.

Narrow widths

Drag the bottom-right corner to test. The board strip crops from the right; the jar and the menu stay put.

Driving it from app code

window.HiveMindTopBar — activity(ms), setState("idle"|"active"), getState(), setMotion(bool), getMotion(), init(root).

Accessibility

  • The art is aria-hidden; state is announced in words through a role="status" region, never by colour or motion alone.
  • prefers-reduced-motion: reduce freezes every loop; the menu toggle overrides the OS setting in either direction and persists.
  • Nothing flashes faster than about 1 Hz.
  • Menu: aria-expanded, Escape to close, up/down arrows between items, click-outside to dismiss, 48 px target.
